# KeyFlowOS Business Autopilot

## MASTER BUILD INSTRUCTION FOR REPLIT AI

### READ THIS FIRST (CRITICAL)

You are building on an **existing application called KeyFlowOS**. This document is the **authoritative product and behavior specification**. Do NOT reinterpret intent. Do NOT add features that violate the constraints below. Your goal is to transform KeyFlowOS into a **fully functional Business Autopilot** that allows non-technical users to run a business with minimal effort.

This is not a dashboard-heavy app. This is not an educational platform. This is not a configurable ERP. This is an **execution-first, decision-minimizing system**.

---

## 1. PRODUCT VISION

KeyFlowOS becomes a **Business Autopilot OS** that:

* Starts a business from idea
* Executes core business functions automatically
* Maintains and optimizes the business continuously
* Requires user input ONLY for approvals or legally unavoidable steps

### Core Promise

> “Tell KeyFlowOS what business you want. KeyFlowOS runs 80–90% of it for you.”

---

## 2. NON-NEGOTIABLE DESIGN CONSTRAINTS

* User effort per day must be **<5 minutes**
* Max **3 user-facing tasks per day**
* App must **decide first, ask later**
* No long forms, no strategy questionnaires, no feature overload
* Defaults must be opinionated and safe

If a feature increases cognitive load, it is wrong.

---

## 3. CORE SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

### 3.1 Core Engines

KeyFlowOS must be composed of these engines:

1. Business Autopilot Engine (central orchestrator)
2. AI Decision Engine
3. Task Orchestration Engine
4. Automation & Integration Layer
5. Analytics & Optimization Engine

All modules below plug into the Business Autopilot Engine.

---

## 4. USER ONBOARDING (≤3 MINUTES)

### Required Inputs ONLY:

* Free-text business intent
* Location (auto-detected, editable)
* Budget range slider
* Time commitment slider
* Solo vs team

### System Responsibilities:

* Infer industry
* Assign business archetype
* Select revenue model
* Generate execution roadmap
* Activate autopilot mode automatically

No additional questions unless legally required.

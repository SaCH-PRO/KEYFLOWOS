What business flows actually look like

Most businesses run on a few loops.

Lead loop

someone sees content or ad
sends DM / fills form / books call
team responds
lead is qualified
quote or offer is sent
lead closes or goes cold

Revenue loop

product/service chosen
quote/invoice/payment link created
payment happens or stalls
reminders sent
service delivered
upsell or renewal happens later

Delivery loop

payment or booking triggers work
files/assets are collected
tasks are assigned
milestones are approved
final output is delivered

Retention loop

follow-up message
review request
educational content
check-in
renewal / rebooking / upsell

Marketing loop

idea -> asset -> approval -> publishing -> analytics -> next content decision

SEO loop

search intent identified
pages/content created
technical/site issues fixed
rankings and clicks tracked
conversion performance reviewed
next content or optimization chosen

The biggest risks to avoid

The repo audit already identifies the main architectural danger: the platform has real breadth and real event architecture, but it still feels like separate apps sharing a sidebar, with too many AI entry points and weak surface-level continuity across modules

So avoid these mistakes:

adding integrations before shared contracts exist
keeping separate AI surfaces per module instead of one command model
letting the same entity live in multiple modules with no clear owner
making the marketplace/integration layer look real before the API/action model is real
building reports that are disconnected from source-of-truth state

You’re right — I misframed it around **Caribbean Hearing Solutions** because that was our prior campaign context.

Let’s realign:

## This CRM/database system is for **KeyFlowOS** itself.

That changes the objective.

You are not only building a contact database for one clinic. You are building a **core business operating system module** that any KeyFlowOS user/business can use to leverage:

**people → relationships → tasks → communication → revenue → analytics → projections.**

So the correct framing is:

# KeyFlowOS Contact Intelligence System

## Purpose

KeyFlowOS should help a business owner manage and grow through relationship intelligence.

It should not just store contacts.

It should help users answer:

* Who do I know?
* What relationship do I have with them?
* What do they need?
* What stage are they in?
* What should I do next?
* Who can generate money, introductions, opportunities, referrals, or execution support?
* Which relationships are improving, stalling, or being neglected?
* What trends can be used to make better business decisions?

# 3. Leverage scoring

This is the big KeyFlowOS differentiator.

Each contact should be scored across:

## Money leverage

Can this person generate revenue, investment, sales, deals, or paid work?

## Time leverage

Can this person save time, perform tasks, automate work, fulfill services, or reduce owner workload?

## People leverage

Can this person introduce the user to customers, talent, suppliers, partners, or opportunities?

## Knowledge leverage

Can this person provide expertise, insight, mentorship, or strategic information?

## Trust strength

How strong is the relationship?

## Urgency / timing

Is now the right time to act?

This becomes a **relationship opportunity score**.

# Finance, Payments & Money Movement

> This slice is where KEYFLOWOS turns commercial documents into money and money into accounting. It owns three layers that mostly (but not entirely) stack: a **gateway layer** that takes card/PayPal/WiPay payments against invoices, storefront orders and event tickets; a **double-entry ledger** (`ChartOfAccount` → `FinancialTransaction` → `LedgerEntry`) that every monetary event is supposed to be written into exactly once through a single `PostingService`; and a **read layer** (trial balance, P&L, cashflow, balance sheet, aging, tax) that derives every reported figure from the ledger rather than from source rows. Around that core sit the operational money modules: expenses and bills (AP), bank statement import/matching/reconciliation, tax liabilities, fixed assets, credit notes, accounting periods, payroll, time-tracking→invoice, contracts, change orders and a generic approvals engine.

## How it works

**The ledger is the spine, and it has exactly one door.** [posting.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/posting.service.ts) is the only sanctioned writer of `LedgerEntry`. `post()` takes a batch of entries, runs a pure `validatePosting()` (≥2 entries, debit XOR credit per line, non-negative, sum(dr)==sum(cr) within `0.0001`), proves every `accountId` and the optional `contactId` belong to the posting business — deliberately with `skipTenantIsolation(...)` so the cross-tenant row is *visible enough to be rejected* rather than silently filtered — then writes a `FinancialTransaction` with a deterministic `externalRef` of `sourceType:sourceId:kind` and its child `LedgerEntry` rows in one commit. Idempotency is the `@@unique([businessId, externalRef])` index: a pre-check short-circuits, and a P2002 race is caught and resolved to the existing transaction. `reverse()` never mutates or deletes; it writes a mirrored offsetting transaction linked by `reversalOfId`, and refuses if any entry carries `lockedByReconciliationId`.

**Recipes sit above the door.** [revenue-posting.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/revenue-posting.service.ts) owns invoice/payment/refund → ledger, branching on `Business.accountingBasis`: on ACCRUAL an invoice reaching SENT/PENDING posts Dr AR / Cr Revenue (split per `Product.category` into `REVENUE_SERVICE`/`REVENUE_PRODUCT`/`REVENUE_PACKAGE`, with drift pushed onto the largest split), a payment posts Dr deposit + Dr `EXPENSE_BANK_FEES` / Cr AR; on CASH the invoice events are no-ops and payments credit Revenue directly. [expense-posting.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/expense-posting.service.ts) is the mirror for AP: `onExpensePaid`, `onBillCreated`, `onBillPaid`, `onPurchaseOrderReceived`, `onProductSold` (COGS at weighted-average landed cost). Both accept a `Prisma.TransactionClient` so callers post inside the same `$transaction` as the source-row write — [expenses.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/expenses/expenses.service.ts) (lines 150-196) and [invoice-workflow.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/commerce/invoice-workflow.service.ts) (lines 216-236) both do this, so a posting failure rolls the business row back.

**Money comes in through three gateways, all in one 1,638-line service.** [payments.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/payments/payments.service.ts) implements Stripe (raw `fetch` against `api.stripe.com/v1/checkout/sessions`, no SDK), PayPal (`@paypal/paypal-server-sdk` for order create/capture, raw `fetch` for webhook verification), and WiPay (form-POST to `tt.wipayfinancial.com`, MD5 hash callback). Credentials resolve per-business from `Business.metaData` (`stripeSecretKey`, `paypalClientId/Secret/WebhookId`, `wipayApiKey`, `wipayAccountNumber`) with env fallback. Checkout is gated by `assertInvoicePayable()` — DRAFT is rejected outright, PAID/VOID are terminal — and Stripe/store return URLs are validated against `PUBLIC_URL_ALLOWLIST`, failing closed if none is configured.

**Webhook idempotency is two-layered.** Stripe signatures are verified with a hand-rolled HMAC-SHA256 over `${t}.${payload}` using `timingSafeEqual`, preferring `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` and falling back to a per-business secret once the invoice resolves. PayPal calls `/v1/notifications/verify-webhook-signature` with per-business credentials resolved *before* verification. WiPay recomputes `md5(transaction_id + order_id + total + status)` and treats a missing hash on a success callback as forgery. Every verified event then goes through `invoiceWorkflow.assertNewProviderEvent(provider, eventId, ...)`, which INSERTs into `WebhookEvent` and reads P2002 as "replay — ack and do nothing"; underneath that, `Payment.providerPaymentId` is `@unique` so the same charge can never produce two rows. Successful payments are written by `createPaymentWithPosting()`, which creates the `Payment` and calls `onPaymentRecorded` in one transaction; refunds go through `createRefundWithPosting()` (negative amount + reversal of the original posting). Afterwards `reconcileFromPayments(invoiceId)` recomputes the balance from *all* payment rows and decides PARTIALLY_PAID vs PAID — partial payments accumulate deliberately.

**Reads never sum source rows.** [ledger-balance.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/ledger-balance.service.ts) produces the trial balance and general ledger straight from `LedgerEntry` in `Prisma.Decimal`. [ledger-reporting.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/reports/ledger-reporting.service.ts) builds P&L, cashflow (with an indirect-method accrual reconciliation: ΔCash = NetIncome − ΔAR + ΔAP + ΔTaxPayable), balance sheet, tax summary and revenue/expense-by-dimension on top of it, and [receivables.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/receivables.service.ts) deliberately computes AR aging *twice* — from invoice rows and from the ledger — and reports the delta so drift is visible rather than hidden. [accountant-export.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/accountant-export.service.ts) packages all of it into a ZIP of CSVs and PDFs using two hand-rolled builders, [zip-builder.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/zip-builder.ts) and [pdf-builder.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/pdf-builder.ts), with no third-party dependency.

**Bank intake is a pipeline with a nightly cron.** [bank-statement-parsers.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/bank-statement-parsers.ts) sniffs OFX/QIF/MT940/CSV from *content* not filename and reports `exactDedupe` honestly (true only when the format carries a bank-assigned id on every row). [statement-source.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/statement-source.service.ts) sweeps Google Drive folders and Gmail queries at `0 5 * * *`, feeds [bank-import.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/bank-import.service.ts), and writes a health row to the awareness feed when a source breaks or goes quiet. [bank-matching.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/bank-matching.service.ts) auto-matches `BankTransaction` to ledger transactions by amount/date/description-overlap; [bank-rule.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/bank-rule.service.ts) is the only other place that calls `posting.post()` for unmatched rows; [reconciliation.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/reconciliation.service.ts) closes a period by comparing statement vs ledger balance and stamping `lockedByReconciliationId` on the entries.

**Schedulers.** Six background loops run in this slice: two `@Cron` jobs (`0 5 * * *` on `StatementSourceService.sweepAll`, `EVERY_DAY_AT_7AM` on the contract renewal sweep) and four `setInterval` loops — finance intelligence every 15 min, tax-liability rollup every 24 h, recurring journal entries hourly, recurring expenses — plus a 24 h FX refresh in [currency-rates.scheduler.ts](apps/server/src/modules/payments/currency-rates.scheduler.ts). The FIN8 and tax schedulers honour `DISABLE_SCHEDULERS=1`/`NODE_ENV=test`; [recurring-journal-entry.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/recurring-journal-entry.service.ts) (lines 41-43) and the FX scheduler do not. There is no leader election anywhere in the server, so a second replica double-fires all of them; the postings themselves are idempotent on `externalRef`, which is what keeps that survivable.

**The surrounding modules are thinner than they look.** Payroll ([payroll.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/payroll/payroll.service.ts)) generates runs from `TimeEntry` hours × `PayRate` and has approve/mark-paid transitions — but never posts to the ledger and never creates an `Expense`. Time-tracking ([time-entry.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/time-tracking/time-entry.service.ts)) has the one genuinely closed loop: `invoiceUnbilledTime` turns tracked hours into invoice lines through a caller-injected `createInvoice`, refuses to invoice nothing, refuses rate-less entries rather than billing them at zero, and verifies the marked count matches. Contracts store `contractValue` and raise renewal obligations via [contract-renewal.sweep.ts](apps/server/src/modules/contracts/contract-renewal.sweep.ts) but touch no money. Change orders carry an `additionalAmount` that nothing anywhere reads. Approvals ([approval-request.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/approvals/approval-request.service.ts)) is a generic multi-step engine with threshold auto-approval, used by content-ops and the AI flow orchestrator — not by any money path in this slice.

## Data model

**Owned exclusively by this slice** (no reader or writer outside finance / payments / expenses / reports / payroll / contracts / change-orders / approvals / time-tracking):
`FinancialTransaction`, `LedgerEntry`, `ChartOfAccount`, `FinancialAccount`, `BankTransaction`, `BankRule`, `BankConnection`, `Reconciliation`, `TaxLiability`, `TaxRate`, `ExchangeRate`, `FixedAsset`, `CreditNote`, `AccountingPeriod`, `RecurringJournalEntry`, `CashReserveBucket`, `FinanceActionItem`, `ExpenseCategory`, `ExpenseBudget`, `RecurringExpense`, `PayRate`, `PayrollRun` (+ items), `ApprovalRequest`, `ApprovalStep`, `ChangeOrder`, `Contract` (+ `ContractAlert`, `ContractTag`).

**Shared / read across the slice boundary:** `Invoice`, `InvoiceItem`, `Payment`, `PaymentLink`, `Quote`, `Expense`, `MarketplaceOrder`, `EventAttendee`, `Contact`, `Business`, `Project`, `ProjectTask`, `TimeEntry`, `Product`, `ProductCostProfile`, `InventoryStock`, `Booking`, `Service`, `RevenueAction`, `WebhookEvent`, `OrgAssignment`, `Asset`, `DocumentInstance`, `KeyCortexMemory`, `CommandItem`, `TeamActivityLog`, `RetainerAgreement`, `DriveIntakeFile`.

**Precision — the single most important structural fact.** The ledger is `Decimal`; everything upstream of it is `Float`.

- **Ledger side** (`Decimal(18,4)`, per the explicit schema comment at `packages/db/prisma/schema.prisma:9703` — *"All monetary fields use Decimal(18,4) (NOT Float) because rounding drift accumulates over a ledger and breaks reconciliation"*): `LedgerEntry.debit/credit`, `FinancialTransaction.amount`, `FinancialAccount.openingBalance/currentBalance`, `Reconciliation.statementBalance/systemBalance/difference`, `TaxLiability.taxableSales/taxCollected/taxPaid/amountDue`, `FixedAsset.*`, `CreditNote.amount`, `CashReserveBucket.targetAmount`, `ExchangeRate.rate` (`Decimal(18,8)`), `TaxRate.rate` (`Decimal(8,4)`), `PayRate.amount`, `PayrollRun.totalGross`, `PayrollItem.grossPay/hoursWorked`.
- **Source side** (**`Float` = IEEE-754 double**): `Invoice.subtotal/taxRate/taxAmount/discountValue/discountAmount/total`, `Quote.*` (same set), `Payment.amount`, `Payment.processorFee`, `Expense.amount`, `Expense.taxAmount`, `ExpenseBudget.amount`, `ChangeOrder.additionalAmount/additionalHours`, `Contract.contractValue`, `TimeEntry.hourlyRate`, `MarketplaceOrder.total`.

Every float→Decimal crossing happens inside `PostingService` via `new Prisma.Decimal(String(x))`, so the ledger stays internally exact. The drift lives on the source side and in every figure derived from it — see Wiring reality #13.

## Wiring reality

All nine modules are in the `app.module.ts` import graph (`ApprovalModule:140`, `PaymentsModule:165`, `ExpensesModule:170`, `FinanceModule:171`, `ReportsModule:172`, `TimeTrackingModule:209`, `ChangeOrderModule:212`, `ContractsModule:240`; `PayrollModule` is not listed directly but is imported by `AiModule` at [ai.module.ts](apps/server/src/modules/ai/ai.module.ts) line 99, and `AiModule` is at `app.module.ts:155`). Every provider a controller injects is registered in its module's `providers`, and `StripeConnector`/`PayPalConnector`/`WiPayConnector` resolve inside `PaymentsModule` — which imports no connector module — because [connector.module.ts](apps/server/src/core/connectors/connector.module.ts) is `@Global()` (line 43) and registered at `app.module.ts:135`. `ScheduleModule.forRoot()` is at `app.module.ts:132`, so both `@Cron` jobs really fire. The core ledger path — webhook → `Payment` → `RevenuePostingService` → `PostingService` → `LedgerEntry` → reports — is genuinely connected end to end and covered by a live-DB e2e test.

Everything below is broken or unreachable, each verified.

**1. `FinancialAccount.currentBalance` is written once and never updated again.** `grep -rn "currentBalance" apps/server/src --include=*.ts` returns exactly one write site outside test code: [finance-accounts.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/finance-accounts.service.ts) line 78, `currentBalance: opening`, at account creation. `update()` (lines 87–108) never puts it in `data`. `PostingService` writes `LedgerEntry` and never touches `FinancialAccount`. `grep -rn "financial_accounts" apps/server/src` finds no raw SQL. Seven read sites treat it as live cash: [safe-to-spend.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/safe-to-spend.service.ts) line 55, [money-moves.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/money-moves.service.ts) line 132, [cashflow-forecast.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/cashflow-forecast.service.ts) line 87, [finance-overview.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/finance-overview.service.ts) line 206, [finance-intelligence.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/finance-intelligence.service.ts) lines 265 and 594, plus `intelligence/health-score.service.ts:80` and `os/os.service.ts:182`. Every "cash balance", "safe to spend", "cash runway" figure and every low-runway alert in the product is computed from the opening balance — normally `0`. Trial balance and Reports are unaffected because they read `LedgerEntry` directly, which is why the two surfaces disagree.

**2. `CreditNoteService.apply()` throws on every call.** It looks up the original posting with `transaction: { sourceType: 'INVOICE', ... }` at [credit-note.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/credit-note.service.ts) lines 111 and 124. Every writer uses the mixed-case `'Invoice'` — [revenue-posting.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/revenue-posting.service.ts) line 160 and [invoice-workflow.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/commerce/invoice-workflow.service.ts) line 160 — and every other reader agrees ([receivables.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/receivables.service.ts) line 169, [ledger-reporting.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/reports/ledger-reporting.service.ts) line 260). `grep -o "sourceType: '[^']*'"` across finance/expenses/commerce yields `3 × 'Invoice'` against `2 × 'INVOICE'`, and both uppercase occurrences are in this one file. Postgres string equality is case-sensitive, so `revEntries` is always empty and line 134 raises `BadRequestException('Could not find original invoice ledger entries to reverse')`. The web ships a credit-notes page wired to it: `apps/web/src/app/app/finance/credit-notes/page.tsx:71` → `apps/web/src/lib/api/finance.ts:187`. There is no `credit-note.service.spec.ts`. Related: `void()` (line 214) only flips status — it never reverses an applied posting.

**3. `capturePaypalOrder` records a SUCCESSFUL payment with no ledger entry, and poisons the webhook that would have posted it.** [payments.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/payments/payments.service.ts) line 1459 calls `this.prisma.client.payment.create(...)` directly instead of the `createPaymentWithPosting` wrapper used on every webhook path (lines 466, 863, 1112). The later `PAYMENT.CAPTURE.COMPLETED` webhook keys on the same `captureId`, hits `if (existing) return;` at line 830 and returns before posting. So the synchronous PayPal capture flow — the one the browser drives via `POST /payments/paypal/capture/:orderId` — books revenue that never reaches the ledger, permanently.

**4. `PaymentsOpsService.refundCharge` records a refund with no ledger reversal.** [payments-ops.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/payments/payments-ops.service.ts) line 156 creates the negative `Payment` row with a bare `payment.create`, never calls `revenuePosting.onPaymentRefunded`, and never calls `reconcileFromPayments` — so the invoice does not re-open either. The provider webhook that follows *would* repair both, except the row it would create is deduped by `providerPaymentId: refund.id` (lines 532 and 889) and it returns early. `POST /payments/businesses/:businessId/refunds` therefore silently diverges the ledger from the payments table.

**5. Storefront Stripe checkout violates a foreign key on every call.** [payments.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/payments/payments.service.ts) line 1373 writes `invoiceId: input.orderId`, where `orderId` is a `MarketplaceOrder.id` — passed as `order.id` from [site.controller.ts](apps/server/src/modules/site/site.controller.ts) line 192 and from `createGenericCheckout`'s `storefront_order` branch. `Payment.invoiceId` is a required FK: `ALTER TABLE "payments" ADD CONSTRAINT "payments_invoice_id_fkey" FOREIGN KEY ("invoice_id") REFERENCES "invoices"("id")` in `packages/db/prisma/migrations/0_baseline/migration.sql`, and `grep -rln payments_invoice_id_fkey` over all migrations shows it is never dropped. The insert raises P2003 *after* the Stripe session has already been created, so the customer gets a 500 with a live checkout session orphaned upstream. The `CASH`/`MANUAL` branch at line 1240 has the same defect but is unreachable (both callers exclude those methods).

**6. `GET /businesses/:businessId/approvals/pending-for-me` is shadowed and can never run.** `@Get('businesses/:businessId/approvals/:approvalId')` is declared at [approval.controller.ts](apps/server/src/modules/approvals/approval.controller.ts) line 61; `pending-for-me` at line 121. Express matches in registration order, so the request lands in `getById(approvalId: 'pending-for-me')`. The web calls it from `apps/web/src/lib/api/approvals.ts:49`.

**7. Three more approvals contract mismatches, all reachable through the live `ValidationPipe`** (`whitelist: true, transform: true`, [app-bootstrap.ts](apps/server/src/app-bootstrap.ts) line 27 — this is the only `useGlobalPipes` in the server, and `main.ts` calls `configureNestApp`):
- `DecideApprovalStepDto.decision` is `@IsIn(['approve','reject']) @IsNotEmpty()`, yet the controller hardcodes the decision and never reads `body.decision`. The web sends `{ comment }` on approve and `{ reason }` on reject with no `decision` (`apps/web/src/lib/api/approvals.ts:82,93`) → 400 on every approve and every reject.
- `DelegateApprovalStepDto` requires `delegatedTo`; the web sends `delegateTo` (`approvals.ts:104`) → stripped by whitelist, then 400.
- `cancelApprovalRequest` issues `DELETE .../approvals/:id/cancel` (`approvals.ts:124`); the server has only `DELETE .../approvals/:approvalId` → 404. Called from `apps/web/src/app/app/approvals/[id]/page.tsx:132`.

[approval.controller.spec.ts](apps/server/src/modules/approvals/approval.controller.spec.ts) instantiates the controller directly with `{ decision: 'approve' } as any`, bypassing the pipe entirely, so it cannot catch any of these.

**8. `ExchangeRate` is a write-only store, and the ledger is currency-blind.** `grep -rn "exchangeRate\." apps/server/src` returns hits only inside [exchange-rate.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/exchange-rate.service.ts) itself — no posting recipe, report, or balance query ever reads a rate. Meanwhile `PostingService` stamps `currency` on each `LedgerEntry`, but [ledger-balance.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/ledger-balance.service.ts) (lines 59–90) groups by `accountId` alone and sums `debit`/`credit` across every currency with no conversion — so a USD invoice and a TTD invoice add together as bare numbers in the trial balance, P&L and balance sheet. The only live FX in the slice is `CurrencyRatesService.toTtd`, used exclusively for the display-only `amountTtd` column in the payments-ops transaction list. Related: `createPaypalOrder` and the PayPal branch of `createStorePayment` both contain `currencyCode: invoice.currency === 'USD' ? 'USD' : 'USD'` — a non-USD invoice is charged in USD at face value.

**9. `ChangeOrder.additionalAmount` reaches nothing.** `grep -rn "changeOrder" apps/server/src --include=*.ts` outside `modules/change-orders/` returns zero hits. Nothing converts an approved change order into an invoice, an invoice line, or a posting; `status: 'INVOICED'` and the `invoiceId` column are only ever set by a caller passing them in by hand. The web has a `/app/change-orders` page wired to the CRUD routes, so it reads as a working feature.

**10. Payroll never moves money.** `markRunPaid` in [payroll.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/payroll/payroll.service.ts) updates `status` and `paidAt` and nothing else — no `PostingService` call, no `Expense` row, no `Payment`. `PayrollService`'s constructor injects only `PrismaService`. Consistently, `SafeToSpendService` hardcodes `payrollReserved = 0` with the comment *"Placeholder: payroll and debt not yet modeled"*. Separately, `setRate` and `generateRun` have no HTTP route — the controller exposes only list-rates / list-runs / get-run / approve / mark-paid — so a run can only be created through the AI tools `payroll_set_rate` and `payroll_generate_run` ([flow-orchestrator.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/ai/flow-orchestrator.service.ts) lines 5544 and 5553).

**11. Unauthenticated, unmetered payment reads.** `PaymentsController` has no auth guard (the repo has no `APP_GUARD`), and `PublicRateLimitGuard.canActivate` returns `true` immediately when the handler carries no `@PublicRateLimit` metadata ([public-rate-limit.guard.ts](apps/server/src/core/guards/public-rate-limit.guard.ts) lines 26–27). `GET /payments/invoice/:invoiceId/status` and `GET /payments/paypal/setup` carry no such decorator, so any caller holding an invoice id can enumerate that invoice's complete payment history — amounts, providers, provider payment ids, notes, `recordedBy` — at unlimited rate.

**12. Live services with no browser client.** The whole of [bills.controller.ts](apps/server/src/modules/expenses/bills.controller.ts): grepping `apps/web/src` for `payables/aging`, `vendors/balances` and `/bills` returns nothing. The underlying methods *are* reachable — `markBillPaid`, `getPayablesAging` and `getVendorBalances` are called from [flow-orchestrator.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/ai/flow-orchestrator.service.ts) lines 5325–5336, and `voidExpense` from `key-cortex/adapters/finance-adapter.service.ts:148` — so this is an unexercised HTTP surface, not dead logic. Also unused by the web: `GET /finance/businesses/:bid/money-moves` (the service is reached through the `generateMoneyMoves` KEY tool instead), `GET .../receivables/aging`, `GET .../customers/:contactId/balance`, the six `tax/liabilities/*` alias routes that duplicate `tax-liabilities/*`, and `GET /time-entries/businesses/:bid/projects/:pid/total-time`. Every other web call in this slice resolves to a real route: I normalised the templated paths in `apps/web/src` for `/finance`, `/payments`, `/expenses`, `/payroll`, `/contracts`, `/change-orders`, `/approvals`, `/time-entries` and `/reports` and matched them against the controller prefixes; apart from the two approvals mismatches in #6/#7 there are no path mismatches.

**13. Float money math everywhere upstream of the ledger.** The schema comment is explicit that Float is wrong for money, and the ledger obeys it — the source documents do not (see Data model). Concrete consequences: `Math.round(Number(invoice.total) * 100)` converts a double into Stripe minor units ([payments.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/payments/payments.service.ts) line 255) and again for store orders (line 1339); WiPay and PayPal use `invoice.total.toFixed(2)` outbound and `parseFloat(total)` inbound; `computeBalance` sums `Payment.amount` doubles to decide PAID vs PARTIALLY_PAID; `RevenuePostingService.resolveRevenueSplits` accumulates with `+(a + b).toFixed(2)` and patches the residual onto the largest split; [receivables.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/receivables.service.ts) performs roughly fifteen `+(x + y).toFixed(2)` accumulations per aging report; `finance-intelligence`, `money-moves`, `safe-to-spend`, `cashflow-forecast`, `finance-overview` and `reports.service` all reduce Float columns with `+`. `ReceivablesService.ledgerDelta` is the one place the resulting invoice-vs-ledger divergence is surfaced rather than hidden.

**14. Scheduler hygiene.** `RecurringJournalEntryService.onModuleInit` ([recurring-journal-entry.service.ts](apps/server/src/modules/finance/recurring-journal-entry.service.ts) lines 41–43) and `CurrencyRatesScheduler.onModuleInit` start their intervals unconditionally — unlike the FIN8 and tax schedulers they ignore both `DISABLE_SCHEDULERS=1` and `NODE_ENV=test`. No leader election exists server-wide, so both `@Cron` jobs and all five intervals double-fire per replica. Ledger postings survive that via `externalRef` idempotency; the FX fetch and the intelligence scan do not deduplicate.

## Open questions

1. Was `FinancialAccount.currentBalance` ever meant to be maintained, or is the whole cash-balance surface supposed to derive from `LedgerBalanceService.getAccountBalance(chartOfAccountId)`? Both designs are present in the code and neither is finished. Deciding this changes eight read sites.
2. Multi-currency intent: `LedgerEntry.currency` exists and `ExchangeRate` is a full CRUD surface with a `Decimal(18,8)` rate, yet no conversion is applied anywhere. Is a business meant to be single-currency (in which case both are dead weight and the trial balance is correct), or is FX translation an unimplemented phase? The `=== 'USD' ? 'USD' : 'USD'` in the PayPal paths suggests the latter was abandoned mid-write.
3. `sourceType` has no shared constant or enum — the `'Invoice'` vs `'INVOICE'` split in credit notes is one typo away from recurring. Which casing is canonical, and should `buildExternalRef` take a typed discriminator?
4. `TaxLiability` and `Expense`/`Invoice` each carry a legacy numeric `taxRate`/`taxAmount` alongside the newer `taxRateId` FK. Which wins in `TaxLiabilityService.aggregatePeriod` when both are set, and does `LedgerReportingService.getTaxSummary` apply the same precedence?
5. Payroll ends at `status = PAID`. Should `markRunPaid` post Dr Payroll Expense / Cr Cash directly, or create `Expense` rows that then flow through `ExpensePostingService`? The `payrollReserved` placeholder in `SafeToSpendService` implies someone intended the former.
6. Storefront payments reuse `Payment.invoiceId` for `MarketplaceOrder` ids. Is the intended fix a nullable `orderId` column on `Payment`, or auto-creating a shadow `Invoice` per storefront order (which would also make `RevenuePostingService` work for store revenue)?
7. `WebhookEvent` rows appear to be written and never pruned. Is there a retention policy, and at what volume does the unique index become the ingestion bottleneck?
8. No leader election exists. For the FX refresh and the 15-minute intelligence scan — neither idempotent by construction — is multi-replica deployment actually in scope, or is the API intended to run as a singleton behind the Hetzner Compose stack?

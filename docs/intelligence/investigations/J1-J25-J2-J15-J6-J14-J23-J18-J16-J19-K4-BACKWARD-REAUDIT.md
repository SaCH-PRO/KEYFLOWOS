# 3. J1 — Business Birth

## Pressure

J1 creates the initial business representation through founder declarations, onboarding/genesis, seeded Blueprint data and initial readiness.

## K4 refinement

Birth must distinguish:

```text
FOUNDER ASSERTION
SYSTEM INFERENCE
IMPORTED FACT
VERIFIED EXTERNAL FACT
DERIVED READINESS
```

Initial onboarding completeness is not equivalent to epistemic verification.

Target founding chain:

```text
founder/operator assertion
→ KnowledgeAssertion + provenance
→ initial KnowledgeRevision
→ confidence/verification appropriate to source
→ derived Genome/readiness
```

The user should not be forced through heavyweight verification for ordinary low-risk profile facts. But legal, financial, authority-driving or autonomy-critical knowledge can require stronger provenance/verification before it unlocks material capabilities.

## Temporal refinement

Founding data may be known now but effective later, or entered later as retroactively true. The target should preserve valid-time/system-time distinction where material rather than encoding all truth as `updatedAt`.

## Verdict

`SURVIVES — J1 becomes the first producer of revision-aware business knowledge rather than merely the first Blueprint writer.`

No new finding root required.

# 4. J25 — Human Authority Lifecycle

## Pressure

Who may assert, edit, verify, correct or approve autonomy-relevant business knowledge?

## K4 refinement

J25 must expose authority classes that K4 can consume:

```text
CAN_ASSERT_KNOWLEDGE
CAN_EDIT_LOW_RISK_PROFILE_KNOWLEDGE
CAN_VERIFY_KNOWLEDGE
CAN_APPROVE_MATERIAL_KNOWLEDGE_CHANGE
CAN_WITHDRAW/CORRECT_KNOWLEDGE
```

These are capability/resource/impact scoped. They are not new K4-owned roles.

Target law:

```text
BUSINESS MEMBERSHIP
!= KNOWLEDGE VERIFICATION AUTHORITY
```

Historical verification remains attributed to the principal who performed it even if their role later changes. Current authority controls new verification/correction, not historical evidence rewriting.

## Delegation refinement

A delegated principal can verify/correct only within the grantor's grantable authority and exact knowledge scope. Learning cannot create or expand this delegation.

## Verdict

`SURVIVES — K4 consumes K2/J25 effective authority; no Genome-specific authority engine.`

F162/C112 strengthened.

# 5. J2 — KEY Request → Governed Action

## Pressure

Which knowledge was used when KEY formed and cleared an exact action?

## K4 refinement

Action formation should eventually preserve an explainable knowledge basis for material decisions:

```text
ActionEnvelope
+ relevant KnowledgeRevision references/snapshot identity
+ EpistemicEligibility results
+ current authority/policy
→ Clearance
```

This does **not** require attaching every prompt token/fact to every action. Use impact/risk tiering.

For high-impact actions, the system should be able to answer:

```text
what current knowledge materially justified this action?
was it verified/current or merely contextual?
what later correction changed that knowledge?
```

## Prompt refinement

F176 means model context can contain stale/disputed information for reasoning *about* uncertainty, but it must not be formatted as unqualified current truth.

A model-generated proposal remains only a proposal. Deterministic Clearance revalidates authoritative/current knowledge as required.

## Verdict

`SURVIVES — J2 gains knowledge-basis provenance and consumer eligibility; exact-action governance remains intact.`

# 6. J15 — Approval / Governance Lifecycle

## Pressure

Does correcting business knowledge invalidate historical approval? Does knowledge mutation itself need approval?

## K4 refinement

Preserve:

```text
Historical ControlEvidence
= valid fact about the decision at time T

Current Clearance
= current permission under current knowledge/authority/policy
```

A later knowledge correction does not erase a historically valid approval. It may change whether a not-yet-effective action may still execute.

Target for material knowledge change:

```text
KnowledgeChangeIntent
+ base revision
+ current authority
+ impact/risk classification
→ DIRECT APPLY | CONFIRM | APPROVE | BLOCK
```

Only material/high-impact knowledge changes require governance. Ordinary profile correction must remain usable.

## Correction completion

Where a governed correction has broad active descendants, the decision is not fully operationally applied until required derivatives converge or are explicitly marked stale/incomplete.

Candidate K8 evidence term `CorrectionCompletionEvidence` remains useful but is not yet promoted to a canonical concept ID.

## Verdict

`SURVIVES — J15 governs material knowledge mutation without becoming the knowledge owner.`

# 8. J14 — Webhook / External Event Ingress

## Pressure

Does an external occurrence automatically become business knowledge?

## K4 refinement

No.

```text
IngressOccurrence RECEIVED/APPLIED
!= verified business proposition
```

J14/K7/K9 establish:
- provider occurrence identity;
- processing state;
- provider/external reality.

K4 decides what knowledge assertion/revision is justified from that observation.

Examples:

```text

→ strong provider outcome evidence
→ may resolve payment fact
```

F166/F177 require that provider/process events not become causal learning merely because they are durable.

## Correction/replay refinement

## Verdict

`SURVIVES — J14 is evidence/observation input, not Business Genome truth owner.`

# 9. J23 — Temporal Flow / Long-Running Workflow

## Pressure

How does business knowledge change over time while long-running work remains pending?

## K4 refinement

J23 temporal semantics and K4 temporal semantics are related but distinct:

```text
WorkDefinition / WorkOccurrence / Attempt time
!=
KnowledgeRevision valid/system time
```

They compose at eligibility/revalidation boundaries.

A long-running occurrence may have been created under KnowledgeRevision R1. Before a material future mutation it may need to revalidate against current R2 depending on policy.

Target historical query:

```text
what work existed at T?
what knowledge did KeyFlow know at T?
what does KeyFlow now believe was true at T?
```

This is the key value of bitemporal semantics where material.

## Projection refinement

Temporal Work Projection may surface knowledge-version/conflict warnings but remains a non-authoritative lens. It must not become the K4 truth store.

## Verdict

`SURVIVES — bitemporal knowledge complements rather than replaces J23 work time.`

# 12. J19 — Privacy / Deletion / Exit

## Pressure

How do legal deletion, correction and retention interact with history and learning?

## Re-audit result

Whole-business deletion and fine-grained semantic correction are distinct.

Existing whole-business GDPR purge is a strong seam because it:
- uses database cascade;
- handles known non-cascading blockers;
- verifies no `business_id` rows survive;
- refuses to claim success on partial erasure.

K4 should borrow the **verification property**, not the physical mechanism, for fine-grained corrections.

Target correction/erasure loop:

```text
source corrected / withdrawn / erased
→ classify retention obligation
→ invalidate/retract source evidence as legally appropriate
→ resolve new KnowledgeRevision
→ compute influence closure
→ invalidate/recompute active descendants
→ re-evaluate LearningEligibility descendants
→ preserve required audit history separately
→ verify active convergence
```

If tenant-trained/fine-tuned models are later introduced, K4/J19 lineage becomes the manifest for machine-unlearning workflows; no such platform is justified yet.

## Verdict

`J19 ADMITTED AS MATERIAL K4 CONSTELLATION MEMBER; broader privacy journey remains under-mapped.`

F178/C128 strengthened; no new root.

# 14. Target architecture refinements accepted

The re-audit accepts these refinements into KF-REC-049 direction:

1. **Revision identity is mandatory for material knowledge.**
2. **Valid-time vs system-known-time distinction is semantically accepted where material.**
3. **EpistemicEligibility is consumer-specific but derived from one knowledge state.**
4. **Deterministic control facts require stronger eligibility than advisory prompt context.**
5. **LearningEligibility consumes J18/K8/K9 outcome certainty and exact-enough lineage.**
6. **Broad domain correlation cannot directly mutate unrelated recommendation confidence.**
7. **Corrections/withdrawals trigger influence-aware invalidation/recomputation.**
8. **Material correction completion is verifiable.**
9. **Learning retraction is allowed when its evidence basis is invalidated.**
10. **Historical/audit truth survives correction but cannot remain active truth.**
11. **No adaptive knowledge signal can expand authority.**
12. **No dedicated temporal/RDF/knowledge database is justified solely from current evidence.**

# 15. Candidate innovation properties retained

These remain target candidates inside K4 rather than new standalone canonical systems:

## Epistemic time travel

Explain a governed action using the knowledge revision set that was available when it occurred, while also allowing present-day understanding of what was actually true then.

## Verified Influence Closure

## Adaptive confidence budget

Confidence changes are bounded by:

```text
eligible evidence
× attribution quality
× temporal relevance
× outcome/recovery certainty
× support strength
```

and never mutate authority.

## Contradiction-preserving reasoning

Advisory/model reasoning may consume conflicting evidence with provenance while deterministic mutation gates use stricter current eligibility.

Checkpoint: `KFP-2026-09-17-01`  
Status: **CANONICAL PRODUCT-LAYER SYNTHESIS / PRE-IMPLEMENTATION**

## 1. Product identity

KEYFLOWOS is an AI-powered business operating system whose purpose is to absorb routine digital administration, coordination, monitoring, communication, analysis and execution work while preserving human authority for material decisions, expert judgment, relationship-heavy work and physical/regulated activity.

Original product principles remain load-bearing:
- pre-opinionated Playbooks rather than blank tools;
- AI as operational partner, not a bolt-on feature;
- Business Graph as shared memory/truth fabric;
- Studio for building/configuring;
- Cockpit for running;
- Flow Feed + Flow Graph + Command/KEY as the operating interface;
- public assets as product/viral surfaces;
- modular isolation as build strategy, seamless integration as user experience.

## 2. Canonical product surfaces

### KEY
Conversational/voice business operator capable of understanding, recommending and executing governed actions.

### Cockpit
Daily run surface: priorities, exceptions, Flow Feed, Flow Graph, financial/operational health, approvals, recovery and current actions.

### Studio
Build/configure surface: business setup, services/products, policies, team/authority, connectors, Space, Playbooks, branding, automation and autonomy settings.

### KeyFlow Space
Unified public business platform: website/presence, storefront, booking, events, registration/ticketing, forms, quotes/payments and portal entry.

### KeyFlow Network
Cross-business layer: community, directory, interactive map, entity profiles, KeyFlow Index, matching, sourcing, procurement, B2B fulfilment/logistics and network intelligence.

### KeyFlow Growth Engine
Closed-loop growth layer: market intelligence, brand/audience strategy, content, organic social, email/lifecycle, SEO, paid advertising, lead capture, attribution, experimentation and governed optimization.

## 4. Original experience promises explicitly retained

### Zero-friction onboarding
Target experience should deliver tangible value immediately, preserving the original spirit of:
```text
sign up
→ establish business
→ connect key channel/calendar
→ publish useful public Space/booking surface
→ receive first actionable value
```

### Flow Feed
A chronological/intelligent stream of meaningful business events, exceptions and suggested/available actions.

### Flow Graph
An understandable visual projection of current customer/work/money flows and bottlenecks. It is a projection, not a source of truth.

### Command / KEY
Natural-language shortcut around the tool maze.

### Gamification
Lightweight evidence-based progress/milestones may celebrate setup and outcomes (first booking, first sale, completed profile, automation active). It must never distort financial/operational truth or incentivize spam.

### Virality
Public KeyFlow Space assets, booking/payment/event pages and Powered-by-KeyFlow treatment may create acquisition loops. Flow Marketplace and Network relationships create creator/network loops.

## 5. Automation philosophy

KEY should automate:
- information movement;
- routine communication;
- scheduling;
- follow-up;
- monitoring;
- classification;
- document preparation;
- deterministic digital actions;
- routine reconciliation;
- sourcing research/comparison;
- workflow coordination.

KEY should escalate or require approval for:
- material spend/contract;
- sensitive HR/legal decisions;
- pricing/policy changes beyond delegation;
- high-risk refunds/reversals;
- destructive privacy/retention changes;
- reputation/dispute decisions with material consequence;
- actions requiring regulated expert judgment.

## 6. Product non-goals

- turning every domain into one giant table/service;
- one opaque AI that bypasses domain owners;
- social engagement for its own sake;
- hidden ranking manipulation;
- unbounded autonomous contracting;
- replacing physical/regulatory work software cannot perform;
- creating duplicate ledgers/CRMs/workflow engines.

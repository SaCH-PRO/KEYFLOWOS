# KIGP Tranche — Institutional Intelligence, Plural Values, Commitments

Status: RESEARCH_ONLY
Programme: KIGP-001
Recorded: 2026-10-06

## Purpose
Extend the intelligence genome beyond individual cognition into plural values, conflicting legitimate authorities, institutional memory, commitments, exceptions, reversibility, and long-horizon stewardship.

## Track A — Extracted candidate genes

### G100 — Plural-Value Representation
Decisions should preserve multiple relevant dimensions instead of collapsing immediately into one scalar utility: safety, legality, authority, commitments, user welfare, business value, third-party effects, reversibility, and uncertainty.

### G101 — Authority Provenance and Scope
Authority is not a boolean. Candidate semantic shape:
Authority = principal + scope + action class + constraints + time + delegation lineage.

### G102 — Legitimate-Authority Conflict Detection
Conflicting instructions from legitimate authorities require explicit conflict detection, hierarchy/rule resolution, and escalation when unresolved. Last-write-wins is not a valid authority model.

### G103 — Constitutional Non-Delegability
Delegated authority must not silently become transitive. Delegation depth, scope, and re-delegation rights must be explicit.

### G104 — Reason-Giving
Material decisions should produce an externally useful rationale artifact containing applicable rules, relevant evidence, competing considerations, uncertainty, alternatives, authority basis, and selected reason. This is not private chain-of-thought disclosure.

### G105 — Precedent Without Precedent Lock-In
Historical decisions are relevant evidence, not immutable law. Similar-case retrieval should preserve contextual differences and authority lineage.

### G106 — Exception as First-Class State
Exceptions should have identity, scope, authority, reason, expiry, and audit lineage rather than becoming undocumented branches or silent rule mutation.

### G107 — Reversibility-Aware Reasoning
As uncertainty or impact rises, prefer options that preserve future choice where feasible: draft before send, quarantine before delete, reserve before charge, ask before irreversible mutation.

### G108 — Commitment Memory
Promises/obligations require durable semantics distinct from ordinary fact memory:
promisor, beneficiary, obligation, trigger, deadline, authority, status, evidence.

### G109 — Stewardship Horizon
Long-horizon consequence modelling is valuable, but forecasting power never grants governing power.

### G110 — Institutional Memory
A business needs durable memory of policy rationale, incidents, exceptions, commitments, decisions, failed experiments, important relationships, and operational lessons.

Candidate law: material action admission must consider scoped authority provenance, delegation lineage, conflicts, and reversibility.

### Goal Graph

Material PRs and runtime actions should be able to explain which canonical law/authority/evidence justified the change or effect.

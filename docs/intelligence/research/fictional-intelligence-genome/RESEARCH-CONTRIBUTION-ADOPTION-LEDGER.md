## Purpose
Prove whether research is contributing to KEYFLOWOS rather than merely accumulating notes.

Every material research result must terminate in one of:
- ADOPTED_INTO_EXISTING_OWNER
- MERGED_INTO_DEEPER_PRIMITIVE
- IMPLEMENTATION_CANDIDATE
- REJECTED
- DEFERRED
- NEEDS_EVIDENCE

Research is not counted as contribution merely because it exists in the corpus.

- PR remains unproven until CI/runtime evidence is available.

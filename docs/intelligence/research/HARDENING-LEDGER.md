## Maturity ladder

1. CAPTURED — durable source/idea exists.
2. EXTRACTED — claims/principles are separated from interpretation.
3. REPO_PRESSURE_TESTED — compared with actual KEYFLOWOS evidence.
4. CROSS_DOMAIN_TESTED — survives more than one relevant repository context.
5. CONTRACTED — candidate behavior has a machine-readable or precise contract.
6. PROTOTYPED — executable/offline prototype exists.
7. ARCHITECTURE_PROMOTED — accepted through canonical architecture-forensics.
8. IMPLEMENTED — production/project tooling implementation exists.
9. PROVEN — required tests/negative controls/integration/admission evidence passed.
10. INTEGRATED — normal workflows use it and continuity surfaces know it exists.
11. LEARNING — outcomes feed future selection/routing/proof.

No item may be described at a higher maturity than its evidence supports.

## Anti-fake-green rules

- committed Markdown is not implementation;
- a schema is not an executable compiler;
- an executable prototype is not production integration;
- green tests are not complete proof;
- workflow success does not mean every advisory check passed;
- research agreement does not create architecture authority;
- historical counterfactual success does not prove prospective speed gain;
- one-domain success does not establish generality;
- retrieval projection is not a new source of truth.

## Compounding requirement

For every surviving R&D item, explicitly test whether it can yield:
- architecture law;
- reusable contract;
- shared implementation primitive;
- proof/test harness;
- deterministic tool;
- agent skill;
- workflow automation;
- KEY capability;
- product capability.

Only build the reusable asset when expected future reduction in complexity, work, risk or uncertainty exceeds the complexity it adds.

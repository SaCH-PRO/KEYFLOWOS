# Research Track — Formal Systems, Self-Reference, Ancient Rule Systems & Symbolic Traditions

Status: QUEUED / SOURCE-VERIFICATION IN PROGRESS
Authority: RESEARCH ONLY

## Purpose

Preserve a second research track alongside software-engineering sources without confusing metaphor, historical analogy, mathematics, empirical science, religion, or metaphysics.

## Evidence classes

Every extracted claim in this track must be tagged as one of:

- FORMAL / MATHEMATICAL: derivable or provable within a stated formal system.
- EMPIRICAL: testable claim about humans, software, organizations, nature, or cognition.
- HISTORICAL: claim about what a person/text/tradition did, said, influenced, or encountered.
- PHILOSOPHICAL: conceptual argument not itself an empirical result.
- RELIGIOUS / MYSTICAL: claim internal to a spiritual or theological tradition.
- METAPHOR / ANALOGY: comparison useful for thought but not evidence of identity or causation.
- SPECULATIVE HYPOTHESIS: candidate idea requiring independent support.
- UNVERIFIED: source or claim not yet adequately established.

## Guardrail: analogy is not ancestry or equivalence

Examples of claims that require separation:

- An ancient binary-looking symbol system is not thereby a digital computer.
- A generative grammar is not thereby an IDE/compiler.
- Gematria is not thereby modern NLP.
- Recursive spiritual imagery is not thereby evidence that physical reality executes software.
- Sacred geometry is not thereby a systems architecture.
- A technology metaphor in a spiritual/self-help book is not evidence of computational mechanism.

Analogies may still be useful. Their role is to generate questions and candidate representations that must survive independent mathematical, empirical, historical and repository pressure tests.

# KEY Role Replaceability Matrix

**Scope:** Any/every worker, duty, role, or operation in a typical business, across all tiers (micro, small, mid-market, enterprise).  
**Assessment basis:** KEYFLOWOS modules as of `feat/key-phase-1-organ-maturation` plus the roadmap in `docs/KEY_MIND_SOUL_EVOLUTION_MASTER_PLAN.md`, `docs/KEYFLOWOS_10_OUT_OF_10_CONSTRUCTION_MANUAL.md`, and related specs.  
**Date:** 2026-06-29

---

## How to Read This Matrix

### Replaceability Ratings

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **R — Replaceable** | The majority of routine tasks can be executed by KEY with minimal or no human intervention once current P0/P1 gaps are closed. |
| **P — Partially Replaceable** | KEY can handle a meaningful portion of the workload, but a human must own judgment, exceptions, relationships, or liability. |
| **N — Not Replaceable** | The role requires physical presence, deep creativity, high-stakes judgment, regulated licensure, or interpersonal trust that AI cannot credibly assume. |

### Assumptions

- KEY closes the critical blockers identified in `KEY_AUDIT_REPORT.md` (safety shell, webhook security, rollback, ingestion model coherence, Genome data integrity).
- The business uses KEYFLOWOS modules relevant to the role (CRM, finance, bookings, projects, connectors, etc.).
- “Replacement” means automation of the *routine digital workload*, not necessarily the elimination of every human touchpoint.

## Final Takeaway

KEY’s biggest opportunity is replacing the **“administrative glue”** that exists in every business tier:

- Micro/small business: data entry, scheduling, invoicing, reminders, lead routing, expense capture.
- Mid-market: the same, plus contract administration, vendor onboarding, ticket triage, reporting, and workflow coordination.
- Enterprise: narrow automation of high-volume, rule-based tasks across shared services (AP/AR, L1 support, procurement admin, HR records).

It will not replace professionals who carry liability, physical workers, or relationship owners. But it can absorb enough of the routine workload to let a business run leaner or redeploy humans to higher-value work.

# Connector Stack Census (`RR-CONNECTOR-STACK-CENSUS`)

- **Decisions:** D-261, D-262, D-141, D-138
- **Baseline:** post-M79 `main` (`2db5e0879`, 2026-11-27)
- **Enforcement:** `apps/server/src/core/connectors/connector-stack-census.spec.ts`
- **Status:** Read-only first slice. No behaviour or schema change.

## Two Coexisting Connector Substrates

1. **Core Connector Registry (`core-registry`)**: `ConnectorRegistryService` (`apps/server/src/core/connectors/connector-registry.service.ts`), `/connectors` routes (`connector.controller.ts`), `ConnectorStatus` (`connector_statuses`), and implementations under `apps/server/src/core/connectors/implementations/`.
2. **KEY Connector Stack (`key-connector`)**: `KeyConnectorService` and `ProviderRegistryService` (`apps/server/src/modules/key-connector/`), `/key-connector` routes (`key-connector.controller.ts`), and KEY Cortex organ adapter (`key-cortex/organs/key-connector-adapter.service.ts`). Note: `IntegrationConnection.authData` is **unencrypted JSON at rest** (not in Prisma's `ENCRYPTED_FIELDS`), and `apps/server/src/modules/key-connector/sync/sync-engine.service.ts` plus `apps/server/src/modules/integration-hub/integration-hub.service.ts` also query `db.integrationConnection` directly without importing `KeyConnectorService` or `ProviderRegistryService` — track them in the later stack retirement slice.
3. **Direct `ConnectorStatus` Accessors (`direct-connector-status`)**: Implementations and domain services reading/writing `prisma.client.connectorStatus` (or raw SQL `connector_statuses`) directly outside `ConnectorRegistryService`.

Per `packages/shared/src/v1-modules.ts` (`NAV-B-HIDE`), payment gateways (`stripe`, `paypal`, `wipay`) and `gmail` support active v1 journeys (D/E/F/G/K); `whatsapp` is a V1.1 deferred module that remains active for businesses with legacy setup; other external connectors (`quickbooks`, `xero`, `mailchimp`, `klaviyo`, `google_*`, `outlook_contacts`, `meta_social`, `shopify`, form platforms) are hidden in v1 unless a business already has legacy data.

# KEYFLOWOS Monorepo

## Overview
KEYFLOWOS is an AI-powered operating system designed to automate operations for service businesses, aiming for 80-90% automation. It provides pre-built Playbooks, a unified "Command" center with an AI-powered command bar, voice input, and integrated business intelligence. The system integrates six core engines: LaunchFlow, OperateAI, GrowthStack, ProfitLens, ScaleHub, and MasterClass Mode, offering a comprehensive solution for business automation, growth, and management. Its business vision is to provide a comprehensive, AI-driven platform for business automation, growth, and management, with a high degree of automation and pre-opinionated workflows.

## User Preferences
- Caribbean localization (TTD currency, Trinidad timezone)
- Refined dark theme with selective glassmorphism (Linear/Stripe/Notion-inspired)
- Event-driven architecture for automation
- Pre-opinionated flows (Playbooks)

## System Architecture
The project is a monorepo utilizing a Next.js 16 frontend (`apps/web`) and a NestJS backend API (`apps/server`), with PostgreSQL managed by Prisma. The backend uses SWC for transpilation, requiring explicit `@Inject(Token)` decorators for NestJS constructor injection.

**UI/UX Decisions:**
- Redesigned design system with a warm Caribbean color palette (orange `#F97316` primary, teal `#14B8A6` secondary), selective glassmorphism, clean elevation system, and PWA capabilities.
- All listing/grid views must be compact, minimizing vertical height and scrolling. Hover reveals additional info, click opens full detail interface.
- UI elements, unified design language, enhanced data tables, and accessible design with ARIA compliance.
- Product detail and form components emphasize inline editing, real-time previews, validation, and performance.
- Standardized shared component library and consolidated shell design.

**Technical Implementations & Features:**

Features a 5-tier weighted completeness system, progressive deepening prompts, and capability unlock mapping. Includes a Business Builder intake wizard.
- **Revenue Workspace:** Restructured into 3-mode tabbed layout (Operations, Catalog, Setup). Features a 6-metric revenue command strip, triage-driven action queue, inline status summaries per section, product segmentation, and readiness indicators.
- **Billing & Payment:** Multi-method payments, shareable PaymentLinks, subscription billing, quote-to-invoice workflows, and expense tracking.
- **User & Security:** Multi-Tenant System with `businessId` isolation, personalized authentication, notification system, global input sanitization, DTO validation, rate limiting, and AI prompt injection guards.
- **Clients Workspace:** Comprehensive client management with metrics, priority queue, enhanced list views, context-aware communication, stage-change workflow panels, and activity timelines. Includes an upgraded AI Priority Queue Brain.
- **Calendar Workspace:** Restructured into Schedule, Performance, and Setup tabs. Features include KPI strips, utilization charts, service demand rankings, staff load cards, AI insights, and a setup builder.
- **Content Workspace:** Full content OS with 3 top modes (Create & Schedule, Calendar, Audiences & Forms) and 4 submodes (Compose, Campaigns, Posts, Scheduled). Features include: social composer with Objective/Audience/Tone selectors and AI Actions panel, Content Readiness indicator, Content Intelligence Strip, Business Pulse Strip, calendar density indicators, scheduled submode with timing conflict/gap/cadence detection and AI best-time suggestions, audience health with 4-tier states, segment quick actions, lead forms.
- **Cross-Module Intelligence Agents:** Client Momentum, Campaign Intelligence, Financial Copilot, and an event-driven workflow engine.
- **Public Storefront (`/book/[slug]`):** Premium, mobile-first, conversion-optimized storefront with section-based layout, product detail pages, category navigation, and a Store Setup Wizard.
- **Store Dashboard:** Flagship brand-aware e-commerce dashboard with live stat cards, catalog preview, QR code generation, and accordion-based storefront configuration.
- **Financial Copilot Agent:** Monitors revenue, expenses, and cash flow with anomaly detection and weekly AI-powered briefings.
- **Customer Notification System:** Transactional email system for branded customer-facing emails with delivery status dashboard.
- **Platform Features:** Gamification, online store, public booking page, module event bus, keyboard shortcuts, webhook dispatcher, and developer settings.
- **Observability:** Request correlation ID, logging interceptor, and health checks.
- **System Diagnostics & Health Center:** Real-time on-demand diagnostics dashboard at `/admin/system`.
- **Learn Module:** MasterClass functionality with My Learning, Catalog, and Certificates.
- **Profile & Intelligence Workspace:** Five-mode layout (Overview / Business / Professional / Documents & Intelligence / Security & Preferences). Redesigned as identity and intelligence foundation layer with outcome-linked forms and cross-module impact visualization.
- **Universal Export System:** All AI-generated content is exportable.
- **Business Documentation Engine:** AI-powered document generation, versioning, and health tracking across 18 categories and 96 document types, with risk-tiered editing and a Document Blueprint System.
- **Navigation & Command System:** Sidebar, breadcrumbs, Command Palette (⌘K), and persistent AI Copilot quick-action chips.
- **Projects Workspace:** Premium delivery execution workspace with 3-tab layout (Board, List, Templates). Features Delivery Pulse execution summary strip, 6-stage kanban board, rich project cards with risk detection, linked client/invoice/booking chips, progress bars, overdue task indicators, search filtering, and sortable list view. Full 10-tab project detail workspace. AI hub with 6 tools and intelligent suggestions. Reusable project templates; auto-project creation from invoice payment.
- **Flows Workspace:** Intelligent orchestration surface with 3-tab layout (My Flows, Templates, Activity Log). Features Flow Health Strip, Flow Coverage Map, Recommended Flows, upgraded flow cards, redesigned Playbook Builder, enriched Template Gallery, and Execution Diagnostics. 19+ strategic automation templates.
- **Automation Playbook Templates:** 19+ pre-built automation recipes across all modules with one-click activation, enriched with business context, complexity ratings, and module coverage metadata.
- **Error Boundaries:** Dedicated `error.tsx` boundaries for all core modules.
- **Security: PublicRateLimitGuard:** Reusable rate-limiting guard applied to all unauthenticated endpoints.
- **KeyflowOS Store Quick-Access:** Global sidebar icon with slide-out drawer showing 3-tier Business Progression System (Build, Grow, Scale).
- **Communications Delivery Engine (Mission 1 — Task #74/#75):** Unified outbound publishing system with 6 Prisma models (ChannelConnection → ChannelDestination → OutboundContent → OutboundVariant → OutboundDelivery → DeliveryEvent). Adapter-based architecture with `AdapterRegistryService` resolving META (Facebook Page + Instagram Business via MetaAdapter) and GOOGLE (Gmail via EmailAdapter) providers. `DeliveryQueueService` polls every 30s for due deliveries, executes through adapters, records `DeliveryEvent` audit trail, and supports exponential backoff retry (60s × 2^retryCount). Full REST API at `/communications/` for connections, destinations, content, variants, publish-now, schedule, reschedule, cancel, retry, and delivery summary. Event bus integration emits `content.published`, `content.failed`, `delivery.completed`, `delivery.failed`. Status lifecycle: Draft → Scheduled → Queued → Sending → Published/Failed → RetryPending.
- **Unified Composer & Distribution UX:** "Compose once, distribute intelligently" experience in the Content workspace. 3-step workflow (Compose → Distribute → Review & Send). Components: `UnifiedComposer` (master editor with content type routing, objective/audience/tone selectors, AI Actions panel), `ChannelSelector` (multi-select destination picker), `ChannelVariantsPanel` (per-channel variant editors), `ChannelPreviewPanel` (live visual previews). Full communications API client. Cross-module entry point via `useOpenComposer` hook.
- **Fulfillment Engine & Order Routing (Mission 2 — Task #81):** Intelligent fulfillment orchestration for all major fulfillment models. New `FulfillmentRoute` Prisma model linking orders to fulfillment sources. New `FulfillmentRoutingService` implementing: LOCAL_STOCK (inventory reservation + decrement on ship + reorder alerts), DROPSHIP (auto-generate PurchaseOrder to linked supplier), PREORDER (accept with lead time, deferred fulfillment, optional deposit), HYBRID (local stock → supplier PO → preorder fallback chain), MANUAL (flag for user action), SERVICE (service fulfillment). Expanded `MarketplaceOrder` status machine: placed → awaiting_payment → paid → awaiting_fulfillment → routed → supplier_ordered → picking_packing → shipped → delivered → completed (plus cancelled, refunded). `MarketplaceListing` now carries `fulfillmentStrategy` + supplier fields + `depositPercent`. `PurchaseOrder` lifecycle: DRAFT → SUBMITTED → ACKNOWLEDGED → SHIPPED → RECEIVED with timestamped transitions. Inventory alerts API returning LOW_STOCK/OUT_OF_STOCK records when quantity hits `reorderAt` threshold. New REST endpoints: POST `/orders/:id/route`, GET `/orders/:id/routes`, PATCH `/fulfillment-routes/:id`, GET `/inventory/alerts`, POST `/purchase-orders/:id/advance`.
- **Commerce Data Layer & Supplier Architecture (Mission 2):** Added new Prisma models: SupplierConnection (businessId, providerType, credentials JSON, accountMeta, connectionHealth, syncCapabilities, lastSyncAt), SupplierProduct (supplierConnectionId, externalId, rawData JSON, normalizedTitle/description/price/images, availability, leadTime), SupplierVariant (supplierProductId, attributes, price, sku, availability), ProductVariant (productId, attributes, sku, priceOverride, imageUrl, stock tracking), ProductSourceLink (productId, supplierProductId, source_cost, leadTime, MOQ, shippingAssumptions, availabilityState, priority), ProductCostProfile (productId, sourceCost, shippingEstimate, dutiesEstimate, packagingCost, transactionCost, landedCostEstimate, grossMargin, marginBand), MarginSnapshot (historical cost/margin tracking), and FulfillmentRoute (orderId/orderLineId, fulfillmentModel, sourceSupplier, warehouse, leadTime, shippingMethod, trackingState, status, fallbackLogic JSON, costSnapshot). Product model upgraded with fulfillmentModel, inventoryMode, compareAtPrice. MarketplaceListing upgraded with fulfillmentStrategy enum. PurchaseOrder links to SupplierConnection. Supplier adapter interface defined (SupplierCatalogAdapter, SupplierOrderAdapter, CarrierAdapter). ProductNormalizationService converts supplier products to internal Products. Full CRUD API at /supplier/* and tRPC router at supplier.* namespace. New SupplierModule at apps/server/src/modules/supplier/.
- **Commerce Intelligence & AI (Mission 2):** Full commerce copilot layer built on top of the supplier/cost data architecture. New services: `LandedCostEngine` (computes per-product landed cost from source + shipping + duties + packaging + transaction fees, auto-saves to `ProductCostProfile`), `MarginAnalysisService` (gross margin %, margin band classification: healthy ≥30% / moderate 15-30% / low 5-15% / negative <5%, recommended retail price at 30% margin target), `SourceRiskService` (single-supplier concentration, long lead time, availability flags), `InventoryRiskService` (out-of-stock, low-stock vs reorder threshold, unverified dropship/preorder supplier data), `CommerceIntelligenceService` (AI product copy rewriting for SEO/conversion, AI pricing recommendation with 3 strategies, AI fulfillment strategy advisor, AI product opportunity scanner with bundles/upsells/underperformers), `MarginSnapshotSchedulerService` (daily periodic margin snapshots for all businesses, manual snapshot capture endpoint). New `CommerceInsightsController` at `/commerce/businesses/:businessId/insights/*` with 15 endpoints: landed-costs, margins, source-risk, inventory-risk, overview (Insights tab aggregate), margin-history, snapshot capture, and 4 AI endpoints (ai-copy, ai-pricing-recommendation, ai-fulfillment-strategy, ai-product-opportunities). All new services registered in `CommerceModule`.
